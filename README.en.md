## What is it

**CedPrinter** is an app for **designing and printing labels on Zebra (ZPL) printers**, made for **Cedisa Central de Aço** and open to everyone. It's free, **ad-free**, and available in Portuguese. The source code is private; this is where the installers and published releases live.

It builds the label at the **exact size of your media** and sends it straight to the printer, so size and position always come out right.

## Highlights

- **Visual designer:** text, barcodes (Code 128, Code 39, EAN-13/8, UPC-A, ITF), QR Code, Data Matrix, PDF417, images, icons, shapes, and lines.
- **Real editing:** multi-select, align and distribute, guides, layers, copy/paste, undo/redo.
- **Batch printing:** import a spreadsheet (Excel/CSV) and print one label per row, with serial numbers.
- **Calculated dates:** `{_data+30}` for expiry dates, in several formats.
- **QR wizard:** Pix, Wi-Fi, link, contact, WhatsApp, email, SMS, and map. Codes are **validated** before printing.
- **PDF and image:** print at actual size or bring it into the Designer to edit.
- **Ready-made templates** and your own library, history, and quick label.
- **Direct printing (ZPL):** over the network (IP), USB/Windows, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and USB (Android).
- **Automatic updates**, showing what changed.

## Install

### 🖥️ Windows

1. Download `CedPrinter-Setup-X.exe` and open it.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
3. Follow the wizard and click **Install**.
4. The first time you use it, click the printer button at the top of the screen and pick your Zebra.

### 📱 Android

1. Download `CedPrinter-X.apk` on the device and open the file.
2. If Android asks, allow **installing unknown apps** for the browser or file manager you're using.
3. Open CedPrinter, go to **Printers**, and pick your Zebra (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or USB).

## FAQ

**Which printers work?** **Zebra printers with ZPL** (reference: ZD230, 203 dpi), over network, USB, or Bluetooth. Other brands are not supported.

**Do I need internet?** Only to download the app and updates. Printing and designing labels work offline.

**Does the app collect my data?** No. Your templates, history, and settings stay on your computer. The app only checks here on GitHub for a new version.

**Is it free?** Yes, for personal and commercial use. See the [terms of use](LICENSE) (in Portuguese).

**I found a problem or have an idea.** Open an [issue](https://github.com/arthurbrito-pjt/CedPrinter-releases/issues/new/choose) with the version and, if possible, the printer.
